# Singapore eSIM number retention and 300M vs 500M broadband notes

This note is part of the mobile network troubleshooting guide collection. It groups two practical network decisions that often need a checklist instead of a quick answer.

## Singapore eSIM number retention

When staying in Singapore for work, study, family visits, or longer projects, the safer setup is usually to split identity and data:

- keep the domestic number reachable for SMS verification, account recovery, and important calls;
- use a Singapore eSIM or local SIM as the default data line;
- confirm hotspot support before relying on the eSIM for a laptop;
- keep a short roaming day pass or WiFi fallback for activation problems.

## 300M vs 500M home broadband

A 500M plan is most useful when several people or devices are active at the same time, or when the home has 4K streams, cloud sync, NAS use, large game updates, and peak-hour congestion. If the bottleneck is old WiFi, weak router placement, or a 100M port, the package upgrade may not be visible.
